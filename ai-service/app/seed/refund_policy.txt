Acme Retail — Customer Refund and Return Policy
Synthetic Knowledge Base Document (Public Support Policy)

1. Standard 30-Day Return Window
At Acme Retail, we want every purchase to meet your expectations. Customers may request a full refund or exchange within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date. Items returned under this standard policy must be unused, unwashed, in original manufacturer condition, and accompanied by all original packaging, manuals, and accessories. Proof of purchase, such as an order confirmation number or digital receipt, is required for all return authorizations.

2. How to Start a Return
To initiate a return within 30 days of delivery, log in to the Acme Retail Returns Portal using your order number and email address. Select the items you wish to send back, choose whether you prefer a refund to your original payment method or instant store credit, and download your prepaid shipping label. Drop the sealed package off at any authorized carrier location within seven calendar days of generating the label.

3. Return Shipping Fees and Damaged Items
Returns for change of mind or sizing differences carry a flat $5.99 return shipping deduction, which is subtracted automatically from the final refund amount. However, if your item arrived defective, damaged in transit, or incorrect due to a fulfillment error, return shipping is completely free. Please inspect deliveries upon arrival and report transit damage or missing parts within 48 hours of delivery with clear photographs of the outer carton and product label.

4. Refund Processing Timeline
Once your return parcel arrives at our regional inspection facility, our warehouse team verifies item condition within two business days. Approved refunds are issued back to your original credit card, debit card, or digital wallet within 5 to 7 business days after inspection. Please note that your financial institution may take an additional two to three billing days to post the credit to your statement.

5. Exchanges and Store Credit
If you need a different size, color, or replacement model, selecting an exchange in the Returns Portal reserves replacement inventory immediately once your return label receives its first carrier scan. Store credit vouchers issued for eligible returns never expire and apply automatically at checkout.

6. Non-Returnable Merchandise
Certain product categories cannot be returned once opened or delivered for hygiene and licensing reasons. Non-returnable items include opened software licenses, digital gift cards, custom-engraved merchandise, and items clearly marked Final Clearance on the product page at the time of checkout. Gift purchases returned within 30 days by the recipient receive store credit equal to the original purchase price.
