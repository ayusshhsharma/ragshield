Acme Retail — Limited Product Warranty Guide
Synthetic Knowledge Base Document (Public Warranty Documentation)

1. Warranty Coverage Duration
Acme Retail stands behind the craftsmanship and reliability of our branded products. All Acme Retail branded electronics, kitchen appliances, and smart-home devices include a 2-year (24-month) limited hardware warranty starting from the original date of retail purchase. Acme Retail branded accessories, power adapters, and charging cables are covered by a 1-year (12-month) limited warranty from the purchase date. Third-party brands sold in our marketplace carry their respective manufacturer warranties.

2. What the Limited Warranty Covers
During the applicable 2-year or 1-year warranty period, Acme Retail warrants that the product will remain free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal, non-commercial household use. If a verified hardware defect arises during the coverage window, Acme Retail will, at its sole option, either repair the defective unit using new or factory-certified replacement parts, replace the product with an identical or functionally equivalent model, or issue an Acme Retail store credit equal to the original purchase price paid.

3. Exclusions and Non-Covered Conditions
This limited warranty does not cover cosmetic wear such as scratches, dents, or normal surface fading that does not impair core operation. Coverage is also excluded for damage resulting from accidental drops, liquid spills, power surges, improper voltage, exposure to extreme temperatures, commercial rental use, or unauthorized disassembly and third-party repair attempts. Consumable components, including replaceable filters and rechargeable battery degradation occurring after the first 12 months of ownership, are excluded from hardware defect replacement.

4. How to Submit a Warranty Claim
To file a warranty claim, keep your original Acme Retail order receipt or invoice showing the purchase date and product serial number. Visit the Warranty Support section on our website, enter your order identifier and serial number, and provide a concise description of the hardware issue along with a short diagnostic video or photo if requested. Once our technical support specialists approve the claim, we provide a prepaid shipping label for defective item return.

5. Repair and Replacement Turnaround Time
Approved warranty repairs or replacement dispatches are normally completed within 10 business days after our service center receives the returned unit. Replacement products or repaired components carry coverage for the remainder of the original warranty period or for 90 calendar days from the replacement delivery date, whichever period is longer. Proof of purchase must be retained for all follow-up service requests.
